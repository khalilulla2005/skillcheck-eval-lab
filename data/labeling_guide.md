# Demonstration labeling guide

These are synthetic examples, not real applicant answers. Labels are assigned against the key ideas listed for each question.

- `correct`: conveys the central concept accurately enough for the short prompt.
- `partial`: contains a relevant idea but omits a key point or is too broad to establish understanding.
- `incorrect`: states a material misconception or does not answer the prompt.

The `rubric_terms` column supports a deliberately simple keyword baseline. It is not a complete rubric and will miss synonyms, negation, and context. A real evaluation would need independently reviewed examples and a fuller rubric.
